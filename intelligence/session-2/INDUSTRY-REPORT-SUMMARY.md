## Market Headwinds & Challenges

### Challenge 1: High Customer Acquisition Cost
- B2B SaaS CAC in marine: $3,000-$8,000 per customer
- Long sales cycles (3-9 months typical)
- Sales & marketing cost: 40-50% of revenue
- **Impact:** Requires higher pricing or lower churn

### Challenge 2: Fragmentation & Legacy Systems
- 70% of marinas still use legacy on-premise systems
- Integration complexity slowing migration
- Staff resistance to change
- **Impact:** Migration takes 6-18 months per customer

### Challenge 3: Pricing Power Limitations
- Commoditization of basic features
- Price competition from budget players
- Small marina resistance to monthly fees
- **Impact:** Race to bottom on basic tiers

### Challenge 4: International Standardization
- Different regulations by country/region
- Localization requirements (15+ languages)
- Currency & tax complexity
- **Impact:** Higher development costs

### Challenge 5: Data Privacy & Security
- Increasing regulatory complexity
- Liability concerns (crew personal data)
- Cybersecurity incidents increasing
- **Impact:** Compliance cost: 5-15% of revenue
