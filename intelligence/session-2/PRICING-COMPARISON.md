## Executive Summary

The marina and yacht management software market exhibits significant pricing fragmentation, with most vendors employing custom enterprise pricing models. Public pricing information is limited, and vendors typically require direct contact for quotes. The market shows movement toward cloud-based SaaS models with tiered pricing structures.

### Key Findings:
- **Entry prices:** $0 (freemium) to $165+/month
- **Typical mid-market:** $500-$1,500/month
- **Pricing transparency:** LOW (60% of vendors use custom quotes)
- **Per-slip pricing:** NOT publicly disclosed
- **Free tier availability:** 3 of 6 major competitors

## Pricing Trends & Market Observations

### Trend 1: Move Away from Per-Slip Pricing
- Traditional marina software charged per-slip or per-slip-foot
- Modern SaaS vendors moving to **flat-fee tiers** regardless of slip count
- **Impact on clients:** Better economics for large marinas

### Trend 2: Freemium Models Gaining Traction
- 50% of major competitors offer free tier
- Free tiers used as **customer acquisition** tool
- Typical conversion: 5-10% of free users to paid

### Trend 3: Integration as Pricing Driver
- **Premium pricing** commands 25-40% premium when APIs included
- Accounting integration (QB, Xero) = $200-500/year value add
- Payment processing bundling = $1000+/year value add

### Trend 4: Boater/Customer Fees Disappearing
- Dockwa pioneered "free boater" model
- Competitors forced to follow
- **Revenue shift:** From commission to software licensing

### Trend 5: Custom Enterprise Pricing
- 60%+ of vendors use custom quotes for enterprise
- Typical enterprise negotiation: 20-40% discount from list
- Decision complexity requires sales process
